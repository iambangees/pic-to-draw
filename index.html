Everything is processed on your device — your photo never leaves it.

1Start with basic shapes

Every picture is made of simple shapes — circles, ovals, triangles and rectangles. This step shows your picture boiled down to its biggest shapes. Copy just these shapes lightly on your paper first. Get them in the right places and the rest is easy.

2Sketch the outline

Now draw the main outline over your shapes — only the big lines, nothing small yet. Keep your pencil light so mistakes rub out easily. This is a sketch, it's allowed to be messy.

3Finish the drawing

Last step: follow the full outline with all the details — eyes, patterns, textures, the small lines. Darken the lines you want to keep, rub out the light guide lines from steps 1 and 2… and sign your name. You drew that.
